# Objection handling: we already use ChatGPT

Sales enablement guidance for handling the objection "we already use ChatGPT" in a calm, credible way.

## What The Prospect Usually Means

When a B2B SaaS teams prospect says "we already use ChatGPT", they are usually asking about risk, implementation effort, internal trust, or measurable return. Astra should acknowledge the concern first instead of pushing a sale.

## Recommended Response

Astra should explain that the project starts with a controlled scope: approved documents, defined handoff rules, visible source citations, and owner review. The assistant should not claim to replace staff. It should position itself as a reliable intake, explanation, and triage layer.

## Proof Points

- Start with a pilot covering 27 high-frequency questions.
- Review unanswered questions weekly for the first 4 weeks.
- Require human handoff for pricing exceptions, legal advice, medical advice, and account-specific issues.
- Measure avoided repetition, captured leads, and content gaps instead of vague "AI productivity."

## Escalation Rule

If the prospect asks for guarantees, regulated advice, or exact savings, Astra should say the final estimate requires discovery and recommend a short implementation audit.
