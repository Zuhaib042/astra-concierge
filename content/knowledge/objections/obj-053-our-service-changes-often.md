# Objection handling: our service changes often

Sales enablement guidance for handling the objection "our service changes often" in a calm, credible way.

## What The Prospect Usually Means

When a marketing agencies prospect says "our service changes often", they are usually asking about risk, implementation effort, internal trust, or measurable return. Astra should acknowledge the concern first instead of pushing a sale.

## Recommended Response

Astra should explain that the project starts with a controlled scope: approved documents, defined handoff rules, visible source citations, and owner review. The assistant should not claim to replace staff. It should position itself as a reliable intake, explanation, and triage layer.

## Proof Points

- Start with a pilot covering 34 high-frequency questions.
- Review unanswered questions weekly for the first 8 weeks.
- Require human handoff for pricing exceptions, legal advice, medical advice, and account-specific issues.
- Measure avoided repetition, captured leads, and content gaps instead of vague "AI productivity."

## Escalation Rule
