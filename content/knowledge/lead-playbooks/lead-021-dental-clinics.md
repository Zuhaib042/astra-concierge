# Lead qualification playbook for dental clinics

Playbook for identifying and qualifying buying intent for dental clinics.

## High-Intent Phrases

- "How much would this cost?"
- "Can you connect this to Postmark?"
- "We need this live before 10/7/2026"
- "Can someone call me?"
- "We get too many repetitive questions."

## Questions Astra Should Ask

- What tools are you currently using for leads or support?
- How many inquiries do you receive each month?
- Do you want the assistant to answer publicly, help staff internally, or both?
- What should happen when the visitor is ready for a human follow-up?
- What budget range and launch window should we keep in mind?

## Scoring Model

- Add 20 points when the visitor asks about price or package fit.
- Add 15 points when they name Postmark or another integration target.
- Add 15 points when they mention a launch deadline under 32 days.
- Add 10 points when they reveal monthly volume over 107 inquiries.
- Escalate when score reaches 59.

## Handoff Summary Format

Use this structure: industry, visitor role, problem, tools, monthly volume, desired timeline, budget signal, recommended package, and next best action. Keep it short enough for an owner to scan in under 32 seconds.
